# CTB Research Project

This project was part of the China Thinks Big (CTB) competition. Our team explored how school meals and nutrition could support myopia prevention among students in Jiangsu, China.

I led the team in collecting and analyzing 1,170 survey responses, reviewing 53 books, and selecting 83 recipes. We also compared school menus and vision data from two kindergartens. To put our research into practice, we developed two drinks, organized tasting events, and gave presentations at local schools. We later worked with a school food supplier to improve its menus.

This folder contains our research paper, competition presentations, and recipe collection.
